[~3 min] So picture a Kestrel tech halfway through writing up a job report. Their phone rings. It’s dispatch, or the next customer. They take the call, and the app just quietly saves the report as a draft. No sign it’s unfinished. [Point to the Before card] And where does it go? Into a Drafts folder at the bottom of the screen. Out of sight. [Pause] You can guess what happened. Half-done reports piled up in a folder nobody ever opened. Here’s the interesting part. The Zeigarnik Effect only works in our favor if the unfinished task is visible. The tech would normally feel that pull to go back and finish. But we hid the thing that triggers it. [Point to the After card] The fix puts the unfinished report right at the top of the home screen, with a progress bar and one Resume button. So the next time the tech opens the app, there it is. Finishing takes one tap. Nothing about the report changed. We just stopped hiding it. [Ask the room: “Where does unfinished work go to die in our product?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Good. Let’s break that down by team.
