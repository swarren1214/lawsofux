[~2 min] Three questions, one for each team. [Point to Product] Product, ask “what can we remove?” The hardest part of managing cognitive load is saying no to good ideas, because they’re all good ideas. A trick that helps: treat every element on a screen as having a cost. A new element should either replace something or really earn its spot. [Point to Engineering] Engineering, ask “is any of this noise?” A lot of extraneous load comes from inconsistency, and that lives in code. Two date pickers that behave differently. Buttons that move when content loads. Spinners that jump around. Consistent components and stable layouts take that load off people. [Point to QA] QA, ask “is the next step obvious?” That’s a legitimate bug category. If you, as a tester, have to stop and think about where to tap, log it, even if everything technically works. [Ask the room: “QA, would you feel comfortable filing that kind of bug today? What would help?”] [Take an answer or two.] Next, we zoom in on one specific piece of that mental budget: Working Memory.
