[~2 min] Loyalty programs are the original home of this effect. That’s literally where the coffee-card research came from. On the left, apps like Starbucks Rewards take that paper card and make it continuous. You see the stars filling up toward your next free item. Make progress visible. On the right, Apple Watch activity rings. Anyone here wear one? [Pause for hands] Have you ever gone for a walk at nine at night just to close a ring? [Let someone laugh or admit it] A lot of people do. And that’s the goal-gradient effect taken into health. A ring that’s almost closed is a strong nudge to finish. Both of these work for the same reason. The remaining distance is small, and you can see it. You know exactly how close you are. These are generic sketches, not real screenshots, by the way. [Ask the room: “Where in our product do people have no idea how close they are to done?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Kestrel had exactly that problem in their signup flow.
