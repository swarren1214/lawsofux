[~3 min] So, Kestrel’s settings screen. [Point to the Before card] Over the years it grew to forty toggles. Legacy sync. Beta map. Route v2. Notify v1 and Notify v2. And thirty-two more. Kestrel is fictional, but I bet every one of you has seen a screen like this. Here’s how it happens: every toggle was added to solve one problem, for one group of people, at one point in time. Then the old migrations, the beta experiments and the debug options just never got removed. And now nobody remembers what half of them do. So what did the techs do? They flipped switches they didn’t understand, and then they called support to get them undone. [Pause] Occam’s Razor says keep the simplest version that still works. [Point to the After card] In practice that meant three things. Check the usage data and remove the toggles nobody touches. Pick good defaults, so most people never need to open settings at all. And move the genuinely rare options behind Advanced. Now the screen is notifications, dark mode, large text, Wi-Fi uploads. Things a tech might actually want to change. [Ask the room: “When was the last time we removed a setting? Does anyone remember?”] [Let people answer. Silence is a fine answer here; let it sit for a second.] Let’s turn that into something each team can do.
