[~1 min] Welcome back. Last time we talked about how people act: big targets, fast responses, protecting flow. Today is Module 4, how people remember. And this one’s a little different. It’s about what people bring with them and what they take away. [Pause] They show up with expectations from every other product they’ve ever used, and a mental model of how ours probably works. And when they leave, they take a memory with them, and that memory is shaped by order, by the high points, by the ending, and by what’s still unfinished. All along the way, cognitive biases bend their judgment in pretty predictable ways. [Point to the animation] Look at the grid on the right. There are nine tiles, and watch which ones stay lit.
