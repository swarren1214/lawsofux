Beat 2 · In the wild

Stand out on purpose.

Recommended plans

Pricing pages typically raise and color one plan as “Most popular,” so it stands apart from its neighbors.

Highlight one, not three.

Destructive red

iOS shows destructive options like Delete in red, so they stand apart from every other choice in a menu.

Save contrast for what matters.

[~2 min] [Point to the left card] Pricing pages. This is the textbook commercial use of Von Restorff. One plan, usually the middle one, is raised, colored and labeled “Most popular.” That’s the plan people notice and remember. And it only works because the other plans are plain. If all three were highlighted, none of them would be. Highlight one, not three. [Point to the right card] iOS uses the same effect, but for safety. In a menu full of ordinary actions, the destructive one, like Delete, is red. And only destructive actions are ever red. That consistency is what keeps the signal strong. These are generic sketches, as always. [Ask the room: “How many things on our busiest screen are fighting to be the one that stands out?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Kestrel took this to the extreme. Let’s see what happens when everything is urgent.
