[~3 min] Picture a Kestrel dispatcher at nine in the morning, looking at a board full of jobs, phones ringing. [Point to the Before card] Every flag on the dispatch board got its own bright badge: new, VIP, warranty, callback, overdue. And every one of those badges was added for a good reason. Somebody asked for each of them. But each one diluted all the others. When every row is colorful, the overdue job, the only one that needs action right now, looks exactly as loud as a routine warranty visit. So dispatchers stopped noticing overdue jobs at all. [Pause] [Point to the After card] The fix doesn’t remove any information. Every flag is still there, just as quiet gray text. But only the one thing that needs attention gets color, a border and a stronger weight. And it says “Overdue” in words, so it doesn’t rely on color alone. Make the default calm, and save the loud stuff for what’s actually urgent. [Ask the room: “What on our busiest screen is loud but not urgent?”] [Let a few people answer.] Let’s bring it back to each team.
