Beat 2 · In the wild

Space is a separator.

iOS Settings

Settings separates groups of related options with space, so each set reads as one block.

Whitespace does the grouping.

Search results

Search engines keep each result’s title, link and snippet tight, with bigger gaps between results.

Gaps are boundaries.

[~2 min] Both of these examples group with almost nothing but space. [Point to the left card] iOS Settings sets related options on a shared background block, with gaps between the blocks. You can tell which toggles belong together before you read a single word. [Point to the right card] Search results do the same thing. The title, link and snippet for each result are packed tight, and there’s a bigger gap before the next result. That’s why your eye never mixes one result’s snippet with the next result’s title. You’ve never had to think about it, which is exactly the point. Whitespace is doing the grouping, and gaps are acting as boundaries. [Ask the room: “Where in our product is the spacing even, when the content isn’t?”] [Let two or three people answer.] These are generic sketches, by the way.
