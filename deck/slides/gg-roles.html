[~2 min] Product, your question is “How close do they feel?” Every multi-step flow should show people where they are and how far they have to go. And if they already did a step somewhere else, count it. Give them the head start. Engineering, yours is “Is the progress real?” Think of progress bars as promises. A bar that jumps backward, or sits at 99% for a minute, or adds steps at the end, teaches people not to trust it. And once they stop trusting it, the whole effect is gone. So drive progress from real state, never fake it. QA, yours is “Does the bar ever lie?” Watch progress indicators under bad conditions. Slow network. An error halfway through. Someone hitting back. The bar should always move forward and always tell the truth. If it jumps back or stalls at 99%, that’s a bug. [Ask the room: “Has anyone seen a progress bar in our product do something weird?”] [Let one or two people answer.] Okay, last law of this module, and it’s a big one. Cognitive Bias.
