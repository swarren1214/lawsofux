[~2 min] Von Restorff on Monday morning. [Point to Product] Product, your question is “What’s the one thing?” For each screen, decide the one thing it has to make memorable, the primary action or the critical status, and protect it from competition. Only one element per screen gets to stand out. [Point to Engineering] Engineering, ask “Is emphasis rationed?” A design system with ten highlight styles will eventually use all ten on one screen. Fewer emphasis variants keep the signal strong. [Point to QA] QA, this one is an important accessibility check: “Does it rely on color alone?” About one in twelve men has some form of color vision deficiency, so a standout that’s different only by color might not stand out at all for them. Make sure it’s also different in text, weight, shape or position. [Ask the room: “QA, has anyone tried our product with a grayscale filter on?”] [Let someone answer.] Last law in this module, and it’s about beauty: the Aesthetic-Usability Effect.
