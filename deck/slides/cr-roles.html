[~2 min] Here’s Common Region on Monday morning. [Point to Product] Product folks, your question is “What is one unit here?” Decide what a card represents: one visit, one customer, one invoice. Then hold that line. A card that mixes two units is just as confusing as having no cards. [Point to Engineering] Engineers, yours is “Is the card a real component?” A container should be one shared component, with one set of rules for padding, radius and background. When borders get hand-drawn screen by screen, they drift apart, and pretty soon they’re implying groups that don’t exist. [Point to QA] QA, you’re asking “Does anything leak out?” Region bugs are visual. Long names, missing images and translated text love to spill out of a card or get clipped. Once that happens, the boundary stops meaning anything. [Ask the room: “QA, what’s the longest customer name you’ve seen in our data? Have we tested a card with it?”] [Let someone answer.] Next, we go from boxes to looks: the Law of Similarity.
