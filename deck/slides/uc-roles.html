[~2 min] Connectedness on Monday morning. [Point to Product] Product, ask “Is this a sequence?” Onboarding, checkout, job steps, approvals: any flow where order matters deserves a visible connection between its steps. If order matters, show it. [Point to Engineering] Engineering, yours is “Do lines come from data?” Steppers and timelines should be driven by the real state model, so the line shows actual progress and can’t get out of sync with what’s really done. A connector that’s only decoration will eventually lie to someone. [Point to QA] QA, ask “Does every line mean something?” Lines are strong signals, so a stray divider or border that joins two unrelated sections is a real bug, not a nitpick. And check that progress indicators still match the actual state after someone navigates back or hits an error. [Ask the room: “QA, have you ever seen a stepper say step three while the data said step two?”] [Let someone answer.] Next is a law with a German name that’s really about keeping things simple: the Law of Prägnanz.
