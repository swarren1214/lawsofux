[~2 min] Mental models on Monday morning. Product, your question is “What do they think happens?” The fastest way to find a mental-model gap is to ask a user, before they tap something, “What do you think happens when you tap this?” Every surprised answer is design work waiting to happen. Engineering, your question is “Does the screen tell the truth?” Most mental-model bugs come from hidden state. If the system has states that matter to the user, saved locally, queued, syncing, failed, the interface needs to show them, and in the user’s words, not ours. QA, your question is “What did you expect?” You already live in expected-versus-actual, it’s the heart of every bug report. I’m just asking you to apply it to what a user would expect, not only what the spec says. Write down your expectation before each action, and log the mismatches, even when the system is working exactly as built. [Ask the room: “Where do we have hidden state that users can’t see right now?”] [Let one or two people answer.] Remember the grid from the start of the module, where the first and last tiles stayed lit?
