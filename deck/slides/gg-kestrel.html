Beat 3 · At Kestrel (fictional)

The endless setup

New customers set up their account across six screens, with no sign of how many were left.

Many gave up around the third screen, unsure how much longer it would take.

The fix: show progress, count the account they already created as step one, and list what’s left.

[~3 min] So Kestrel’s new customers set up their account across six screens. Your home, your pets, your plan, your schedule, payment. [Point to the Before card] And on every screen, there was no sign of how many were left. Just “Tell us about your property” and a Next button. [Pause] Put yourself in that customer’s shoes. You’re on screen three. Is this almost over? Are there twenty more? You have no idea. And a lot of people gave up right around the third screen, just because they weren’t sure how much longer it would take. Here’s what I want you to notice about the fix. The flow is still six steps. They didn’t cut anything. What changed is that customers can see where they are. [Point to the After card] Two goal-gradient moves. First, visible progress: “Step 2 of 6” and a list of what’s left. Second, the head start. The account they already created counts as step one. So nobody starts at zero. That’s the pre-stamped coffee card. One caution. Progress has to be honest. If you inflate it, or add surprise steps at the end, you lose trust fast. [Ask the room: “Which of our multi-step flows hide how many steps are left?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Let’s turn that into a question for each team.
