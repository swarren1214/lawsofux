[~2 min] Product folks, your question is “What are the peak and the end?” Take your core flows and sketch the emotional journey. Where’s the best moment? Where’s the worst? And how does it end? Those two moments deserve more design attention than their share of screen time. Design the last one on purpose. Engineering, yours is “Can the ending fail?” This is where reliability matters most. Picture a payment that actually succeeds but shows an error. Or a confirmation email that never shows up. Everything before that could be perfect, and that’s what people will remember. So confirmations and receipts are the parts that most need to be reliable. QA, yours is “How does it end?” And here’s the honest truth about endings: they get under-tested because they sit at the end of long test scripts. By the time you get there, you’re tired. So test the final step on every path, including the unhappy ones. Failures, timeouts, someone hitting submit twice. [Ask the room: “What’s one ending in our product you’ve never seen fail on purpose?”] [Let one or two people answer.] Okay. From how things end, to what happens when they don’t end at all.
