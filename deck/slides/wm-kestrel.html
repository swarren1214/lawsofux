[~3 min] So picture a Kestrel tech getting ready for their next stop. The customer lives in a gated community. The gate code is in the job notes. So the tech opens the notes, reads the code, says it to themselves a few times, and starts driving. Twenty minutes later, after a phone call and a podcast and a left turn they almost missed, they pull up to the gate. And the code is gone. [Pause] So they pull over, open the app, and go looking for it again. [Point to the Before card] And look at the arrival screen. “Access info is in the job notes.” The data was always in the system. It just lived on a screen the tech wasn’t looking at when they needed it. That’s the classic working-memory failure: information shown at one moment, needed at another, with a twenty-minute drive in between. [Point to the After card] The fix doesn’t add any new data. It shows the gate code and the dog’s name, right on the arrival screen, at the moment they’re needed. [Ask the room: “Walk me through one of our flows. At each step, is what the user needs actually on that screen?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Okay, here’s what each team does with that.
