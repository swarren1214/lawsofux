[~2 min] Here’s what Flow means for each of you on Monday morning. Product folks, your question is “What breaks focus here?” Go through your core flows and literally list every interruption: confirmation dialogs, upsell prompts, rating requests, notifications. For each one, ask whether it earns the right to break someone’s focus. Spoiler: most don’t. Engineering, your question is “Can this wait?” Build priority into the notification system itself. Urgent and routine should be different code paths, not the same pop-up with different wording. That’s what makes the Kestrel fix possible. QA, your question is “Did you lose your place?” This is interruption testing, but from the user’s point of view. Start a task, interrupt it partway through, a call, a notification, backgrounding the app, and come back. You should land exactly where you left off, with your progress intact. If you don’t, that’s a bug. [Ask the room: “QA, how often do we test interruptions today?”] [Let one or two people answer.] Alright.
