[~2 min] Product, your question is “What’s left undone?” Find the tasks people commonly abandon partway. Setup flows, reports, long forms. Make the unfinished state visible, show progress and the next step, and make resuming easy. And keep it honest. Never manufacture incompleteness just to get people back into the app. Engineering, yours is “Does progress persist?” None of this works if partial work isn’t saved reliably. Autosave drafts. Keep them across sessions and across devices. And when someone hits resume, land them exactly where they left off, not back at step one. QA, yours is “Can you resume tomorrow?” This one’s fun because you get to abandon things on purpose. Close the app in the middle of a form. Switch from your phone to your laptop. Come back the next day. Is the work still waiting where you left it? [Ask the room: “QA, how often do our test plans include walking away mid-task?”] [Let one or two people answer.] Alright. Zeigarnik is about the pull of unfinished work. Next, the Goal-Gradient Effect, which is about what happens as you get close to the finish.
