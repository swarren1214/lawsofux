Beat 2 · In the wild

The system carries the weight.

Tap to pay

Apple Pay and Google Pay hide card numbers, tokens and fraud checks behind a single tap.

Absorb it so they don’t have to.

ZIP code lookups

Many checkout forms fill in city and state from the ZIP code. The system does the lookup.

Move the work to the system.

[~2 min] Here are two places you’ve already felt Tesler’s Law, probably this week. On the left, tap to pay. Think about everything that goes into paying with a card: the card number, the expiration date, the security code, tokenization, fraud checks. None of that went away when Apple Pay and Google Pay showed up. It all moved from you to the system. What’s left for you is a tap. That’s about as dramatic as Tesler’s Law gets. On the right is the everyday version: ZIP code lookups. A lot of checkout forms fill in your city and state as soon as you type your ZIP. The system already knows which city goes with which ZIP, so making people type it out is asking them to carry complexity we could hold for them.
