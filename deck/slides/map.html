03

How people act

  • Doherty Threshold

04

How people expect & remember

05

How teams make trade-offs

  • Occam’s Razor

  • Parkinson’s Law

One session per module · about ten minutes per law, including discussion

[~2 min] Here’s the map for the whole training. The website lists the laws alphabetically, which is great for looking things up but not for learning them. So I’ve grouped them by what they explain. [Point to each column as you name it] Module 1 is how people decide. Module 2 is how people see. Module 3 is how people act: tapping, waiting, typing. Module 4 is how people expect and remember things. And Module 5 is different. It’s about the principles we use as teams when we make trade-offs. I saved that one for last on purpose, because it’s the one Product and Engineering leads tend to argue about most, and I’d like us to share a vocabulary before we get there. Each module has its own color, and you’ll see that color again on its divider slide, so you always know where you are. Each session runs about an hour to an hour and a quarter, roughly ten minutes per law, and that includes discussion. So block your calendars accordingly. [Ask the room: “Just from the names, which module do you think matters most for your team?”] [Take one or two answers.] We’ll see if you still think so at the end.
