[~3 min] So here’s Kestrel. Picture a new tech on their very first day. They open the app, they’re ready to go, and the first thing they see is this. [Point to the Before screen] “Required training. Video, 20 minutes.” And the funny thing is, the video was good. It was clear, it was accurate. But it showed up before the tech had any context, at the exact moment they wanted to start working. So what happened? Most of them hit “Skip for now.” And then they called support about features the video had explained. [Pause] That’s the Paradox of the Active User in one screen. [Point to the After screen] Here’s the fix. The tech just starts working. The first time they reach a step, a tip shows up. One sentence: “Swipe a step right to mark it done.” They do it, tap Got it, and the tip goes away for good. And the full video still exists behind “Show me how” for anyone who wants it. We didn’t delete the training, we moved it to where people are. Kestrel is fictional, of course. [Ask the room: “Where do we front-load instructions that people are going to skip?”] [Let two or three people answer.] Okay, what each team can do about it.
