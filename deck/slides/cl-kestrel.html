The tech home screen

Kestrel’s home screen showed a promo banner, six KPIs, a map, messages and inventory alerts.

Techs dug through all of it to answer one question: where am I going next?

The fix: lead with a single “Next job” card. Everything else is one tap away.

[~3 min] So picture a Kestrel tech at seven in the morning, sitting in the truck with a coffee, opening the app. All they want to know is: where am I going next? [Point to the Before card] And here’s what they get. A fall promo banner. Six KPIs. A map. Twelve messages. Three inventory alerts. And somewhere down at the bottom, “Next job.” Every widget here was requested by someone with a good reason. Marketing wanted the promo. Ops wanted the KPIs. The inventory team wanted their alerts. Nobody asked what the tech needed at seven a.m. in the truck. So every morning, techs dug through all of it to answer one question. [Pause] [Point to the After card] The fix leads with a single “Next job” card: what it is, when, where, and a button to start the route. Messages and inventory are still there, one tap away. This is about hierarchy, not deletion. [Ask the room: “What’s the one thing people open our app to do? Is it the first thing they see?”] [Let two or three people answer. If people disagree, that’s worth noting by itself.] Good. Let’s turn that into Monday-morning questions.
