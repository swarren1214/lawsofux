[~3 min] So picture a Kestrel tech wrapping up a job. They’ve treated the house, they’re packing up, and they try to close the job. It won’t close. [Point to the Before card] Here’s why. Kestrel showed each job’s steps as six tiles in a grid, all the same size, in no visible order. That screen says “here are six unrelated options.” Nothing on it tells you “Before photo” has to happen first. So techs skipped the before photos and only found out at the very end, when the job wouldn’t close, and by then the house was already treated. You can’t take a before photo after. [Pause] [Point to the After card] The fix puts the steps on one timeline with a single line running through them. The line itself tells you the order, your progress and what’s next. Done steps are green and checked, and the current step is outlined in blue. Same six steps, no new features, just a line. [Ask the room: “Where do we show a sequence as a grid, a list of toggles or a set of tabs?”] [Let a few people answer.] Let’s turn that into a question for each team.
