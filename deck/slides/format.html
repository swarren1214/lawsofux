[~3 min] Every law gets the same four beats, so after the first one you’ll know the rhythm. [Point to each card] Beat one is the law itself: one sentence and one picture of the research behind it. Beat two is in the wild: real products that get it right, or get it wrong. Beat three is At Kestrel. Let me introduce Kestrel. Kestrel is a made-up home-services company. Their technicians run their whole day from a phone app: routes, jobs, notes, closing out visits. I made them up for two reasons. One, you’ll get to know their product across all the sessions, so the examples build on each other. Two, because they’re fictional, I can make their failures as blunt as I want without pointing at anyone’s real roadmap. You will probably recognize some of our own screens in there anyway. That’s fine. And beat four, Your move, is the one that matters most. Every law ends with one concrete question each team can ask in their next review. If you remember nothing else, remember those questions. [Ask the room: “Any questions on the format before we start?”] Okay. Let’s get into Module 1.
