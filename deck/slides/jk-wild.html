Honor the conventions.

[~2 min] Two conventions you use every single day. On the left, the cart in the top-right corner. Most online stores put it there, so shoppers find it without even thinking. On the right, the logo that goes home. On almost every website, clicking the logo in the top-left takes you back to the homepage. [Pause] Here’s the interesting part. Neither of these is written down anywhere official. Nobody passed a law. They emerged because enough sites did it the same way, and now people expect it everywhere. So a store that hides its cart somewhere unusual isn’t being creative. It’s making every visitor stop and think. Same goes for platform conventions, the iOS back swipe, pull to refresh, Android’s back button. Quick note, these illustrations are generic sketches. [Ask the room: “Where does our product break a convention people already know? And what are we actually getting in return?”] [Let two or three people answer.] If the answer to the second question is “not much,” that’s worth a ticket.
