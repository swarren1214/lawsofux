[~2 min] This law cuts both ways, so each team gets both edges. Product folks, this one’s for you. Invest in visual quality, because it buys real goodwill. But never let a satisfaction score stand in for task success. Put both numbers in front of the team, every time. Engineering, your question is “Is polish part of done?” The stuff that makes an interface feel finished, consistent alignment, spacing, transitions, is usually the first thing to slip when a deadline gets tight. Treat it as acceptance criteria, not extras. It’s doing real work. QA, yours is “Pretty, but does it work?” I’d actually say be most skeptical when the feedback is most glowing. A beautiful build with rave reviews is exactly where usability bugs like to hide. Test task completion no matter what people say. [Ask the room: “Engineering, what’s the first bit of polish that gets cut when we’re behind?”] [Let a couple of people answer.] That’s the end of the seven laws for this module. Let’s pull them together.
