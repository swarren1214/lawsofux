The Office ribbon

Microsoft Office groups dozens of commands into labeled regions like Clipboard, Font and Paragraph.

Regions tame big toolsets.

[~2 min] Two examples, and I’d bet everyone here has used at least one of them this week. [Point to the left card] Trello. Every task is a card, and every stage is a shaded list. So you’ve got two levels of common region on one screen: the card makes one task a unit, and the list makes a set of cards a stage. You never once wonder which column a card belongs to. The container answers that for you. [Point to the right card] The Office ribbon is solving a much harder problem: hundreds of commands. Microsoft splits the toolbar into labeled regions, Clipboard, Font, Paragraph, and that does a lot of work. Even if you’ve never used a command before, you can usually guess roughly where it lives.
