[~2 min] Forgive the input. [Point to the left card] Search spelling fixes. This is Postel’s Law at enormous scale. If you misspell something, search engines don’t return nothing. They work out what you probably meant and show those results, often with a “did you mean” prompt so you stay in control. Meet people where they are. [Point to the right card] Address autocomplete. I like this one because it does both halves of the law. It accepts partial, abbreviated, messy input, a few keystrokes really. And it sends back one clean, standardized mailing address the shipping system can trust. Liberal in, conservative out. Clean up on the way out. [Pause] These illustrations are generic sketches.
