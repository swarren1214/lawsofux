[~3 min] So, Kestrel, still fictional. They flagged dangerous dogs with a red banner across the top of the job screen. Seems reasonable, right? Big, red, at the top. [Point to the Before screen] But look at what’s around it. The safety alert and two promo banners use the same shape, the same position and similar colors. It was literally the same component. And after a few weeks of “Refer a friend” and “Fall promo,” techs had learned to skip that whole zone. Their eyes just route around it. Now picture what that means. A tech walks into a backyard, and the cost of missing this banner isn’t a missed upsell. It’s getting bitten. [Pause] [Point to the After screen] The fix changes three things. The alert moves into the job card, where techs are actually reading. It gets its own visual style that nothing else in the app is allowed to use. And Start job stays disabled until the tech taps Got it. [Ask the room: “Which of our critical messages shares a component with something people ignore?”] [Give this one a little more time. Let several people answer.] Let’s turn that into a question for each team.
