The pest icons

At that size, ants, termites and spiders all looked like the same dark smudge.

The fix: simple, distinct shapes in their own colors, always paired with a word.

[~3 min] So picture a Kestrel tech standing in a customer’s garage, phone held out in bright sunlight, trying to log what they found. [Point to the Before card] Kestrel’s pest picker used detailed illustrations as icons. And to be fair, they looked great in the design file at full size. But shrunk down into a grid on a phone, in sunlight, ants, termites and spiders all turned into the same dark smudge. So techs logged the wrong pest. And logging termites when it’s actually ants isn’t a small mistake for a pest company. [Pause] [Point to the After card] The fix is Prägnanz. Each pest becomes one simple, distinct shape on its own color, readable at a glance and from arm’s length. And the icons never stand alone. There’s always a word right under them backing them up. [Ask the room: “Which of our icons only work at the size they were designed at?”] [Let a few people answer.] Okay, here’s what each team can do with this.
