[~1 min] Welcome back. Last time we talked about how people decide, and how every option and every field spends a little of someone’s attention. Today we’re moving from how people decide to how they see. [Point to the animation] Watch the dots on the right. Same 24 dots the whole time. First they read as noise, then three groups, then one circle, and the only thing that changed is where they sit. That’s today in one picture. The first five laws come from Gestalt psychology, which is the early twentieth-century study of how the brain organizes what the eyes take in. The last two, Von Restorff and Aesthetic-Usability, are about what stands out and what earns trust. [If a designer is co-presenting, introduce them here.] This is the most visual module, so expect a lot of “oh, I’ve seen that.” Let’s start with the simplest one: proximity.
