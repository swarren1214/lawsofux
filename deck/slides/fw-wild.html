[~2 min] Here are two sides of flow out in the wild. On the left, Focus modes on iOS. You decide which notifications are allowed to break through, and everything else just waits. It protects your attention from the outside. On the right, games. Games are probably the best-studied example of keeping people in that flow channel. A well-designed game ramps up difficulty as you get better, so it’s never too easy and never hopeless. [Pause] Now, here’s the honest takeaway for people who build work software like ours. We’re mostly not in the business of creating flow the way a game is. Our job is to not break it. That means fewer interruptions, fewer surprise dialogs, and feedback that shows up right away. And quick note, these illustrations are generic sketches, not real screenshots. [Ask the room: “In our product, what’s the equivalent of a Focus mode? Do we have anything that holds back the noise?”] [Let a couple of people answer.] Most teams say “not really,” and that’s fine, it’s an honest answer.
